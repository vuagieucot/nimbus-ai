Nimbus

A fluffy cat, a cozy house, and a lot of things to knock over.

Made by AI from the designer's art pack. Personal fan project for friends.

How to play

  • Mischief scores. Hold E next to a mug, vase, curtain or the sofa. Chain acts for a combo.
  • Don't get caught mid-act. Tuna and Helly see in their light cones. Caught = lose a heart.
  • Hide and climb.Space jumps up one level (sofa, then counter). Up high they can't see you. Tap E at a box or bed to hide.
  • Needs. Eat at the bowl, nap on beds or in sunbeams, rub legs for love. Empty needs make Nimbus grumpy.
  • Sneak outside when a door opens. Beware Tom, fight Cop, chase Chase. Stay out too long and they come looking.
  • Guests visit some evenings: more eyes, but treats and a favorite request.
